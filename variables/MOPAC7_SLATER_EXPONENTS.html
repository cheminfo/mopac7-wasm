    The valence Slater exponents and principal quantum numbers of every element each hamiltonian can draw, read out of MOPAC 7's own block.f and diat.f.

    The keys are the element symbols of MOPAC7_ELEMENTS minus the ones MOPAC parameterises as sparkles — Na (MNDO, AM1, PM3), K (MNDO, AM1, PM3) — which carry a point charge and no exponent, and to which NATORB gives no atomic orbital at all.
